# TwisWua

The official website of TwisWua, a tiger-themed Twitch streamer. It gives fans one place to see when the next stream is, whether she is live right now, and where to find her merch and socials.

---

## Live Detection

- Shows when TwisWua is live on Twitch, checking again every minute
- Replaces today's schedule entry with the title of the stream that is happening right now

## Weekly Schedule

- Pulls the weekly stream schedule straight from her Twitch schedule, so it never has to be updated by hand
- Shows the time and game for each day, in GMT+8
- Counts down to the next stream
- Shows a notice when she is on a break from streaming

## Merch and Socials

- Shows the Tiger Threads merch line with prices and stock status
- Links to her Twitch, YouTube, Twitter, and Instagram, plus a button to join her Discord
